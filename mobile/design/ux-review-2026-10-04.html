BuyWise UX Review

The look is right. The problem is information design: what each screen chooses to show, how big it is, and which words it uses. Every change below keeps Barlow Semi Condensed, JetBrains Mono and the v3 colours. A few places get new jobs for those colours, and the type gets bigger.

The short answer

Three verdicts

Look and feel Strong

Charcoal surfaces, colour-block cards, condensed titles and the cartoon gullak give BuyWise a face no other budget app has. Nothing here needs replacing.

Clarity Weak

The numbers people act on are small (12 px mono body, 10 px labels), sit inside sentences, or appear as percentages of a figure the screen never shows. The most common task, logging a spend, needs a hidden long-press.

Backend use Partial

The app calls 34 of its 35 API wrappers, so the wiring is complete. What goes unused is what those calls return: days left in the month, the profile's income and savings facts, the payee and category filters, and the AI's ability to log, budget and fund goals on request.

Where the app falls short

High

Logging a spend is a hidden gesture

The core action of an expense tracker is a long-press on the chat button. Home has no add button, and the coach mark lives on Activity. Empty states tell people to "hold the + button", which is a badge on the chat icon.

ui/TabBar.tsx · coachOn in (tabs)/_layout.tsx
High

New users start with a wrong balance

There is no form for a starting balance, so the hero number is ₹0 and goes negative after the first expense. New profiles default to INR and Asia/Kolkata, and copy says "give every rupee a job", while the launch market is the US and EU.

ui/TransactionEditor.tsx · lib/budget.ts readyLine · profile defaults
High

The type is too small to scan

Body copy is 12 px, meta 10.5 px, labels 10 px, chips 11 px, and 39 style rules in src/ui sit between 9 and 11 px. Mono runs wide, so these lines also truncate: budget rows cut their status line to one line.

ui/tokens.ts type · ui/BudgetList.tsx
High

Key numbers hide inside sentences

The balance card's balanceSentence carries the month's net, the budget state and the goals as inline chips. The inline-chip style reads well in chat; on a dashboard it turns scanning into reading.

lib/home.ts · (tabs)/index.tsx
Medium

Budget speaks envelope jargon

Ready to assign, Assigned, Available, carried, opening and closing all appear on one screen, plus four equal-weight chips. The bar measures this month's spend against this month's budget while the pill shows available money including carry-over, so a full red bar can sit beside a green pill.

(tabs)/budget.tsx · ui/BudgetList.tsx
Medium

Six controls do nothing

The bell, Auto-assign, voice search on Activity and Payees, the voice button in chat, and the budget-alerts switch. Each looks live and answers with "coming soon". People read that as broken.

(tabs)/index.tsx · (tabs)/budget.tsx · ui/ChatInput.tsx · (tabs)/profile.tsx
Medium

Buddy lives in one tab

The app is AI-first, but the AI shows up only inside Chat. Home has no insight, a budget row has no "why is this over?", and the composer never says you can log by typing "chai 20 cash". The tool results that would prove it worked show only on the newest reply.

ui/ChatThread.tsx · ui/ChatInput.tsx
Medium

Goals and Reports have no front door

Goals opens from a chip inside the balance sentence or the last button on Budget. Reports opens from the small ↗ on the balance card. Neither is where people look for it.

(tabs)/index.tsx · (tabs)/budget.tsx
Low

Search covers only loaded rows

Activity filters the pages already fetched, 20 at a time, and says "found in the 40 loaded so far". The API has no text filter, so a search for an old payee can miss.

(tabs)/transactions.tsx · GET /transactions
Low

Numbers aren't doors

Reports' category rows, payment-method bars, Home's Spend Pulse legend and a transaction's payee can't be opened. Each could lead to the filtered Activity list the URL already supports.

app/reports.tsx · ui/ReportBlocks.tsx
Low

Two "all" controls on Activity

The category pill beside the title reads "all", and the type chips below start with "All". They filter different things with the same word.

(tabs)/transactions.tsx
Low

Goal money floats free of the balance

Contributing ₹5,000 to a goal changes the goal's number only. Ready to assign and the balance stay the same, so people wonder where the money went. This one needs your call (see the questions at the end).

Design direction

Same style, clearer screens

Numbers in Barlow, words in mono

Any figure a person acts on gets its own Barlow line with a small label above it. Sentences become a quiet line underneath. Inline chips stay in chat, where they work.

One question per screen

Home: can I spend today? Activity: where did it go? Budget: which envelope needs me? Goals: am I on track? Reports: how did the month go?

One tap to log

A visible "+ Log" pill on Home and Activity, and a chat box that logs plain sentences. The hold gesture stays as a shortcut for people who learn it.

Plain words first

"Left to budget", "Budgeted", "Left". Envelope terms can live in a help sheet for people who come from YNAB.

Every number opens something

A category, payee, day or envelope figure opens the list behind it. The URL filters on Activity already make this cheap.

Ship only what works

Remove a control until its feature exists. An empty slot costs nothing; a dead button costs trust in a money app.

Colour roles

The palette stays. Each colour gets one job, so it means the same thing on every screen. Category tiles keep their hues; state (over, close, fine) is carried by text colour and pills, so a tomato Food tile never reads as "overspent".

Fonts unchanged. Sizes move up so the smallest everyday text is 12 px. Change it once in tokens.ts and every screen follows.

Onboarding

  • Four short steps on sage, in the Login and Goals look, using the Quick Add amount field and the tomato button.
  • Currency and time zone from the phone. A US phone gets USD and its zone; the profile's INR and Asia/Kolkata defaults stop leaking into a US launch. Copy that says "rupee" moves to the currency's own name.
  • Starting balance. Saved as a starting_balance transaction, which the API accepts today.
  • How you get paid, and how much to save. Writes income_type, salary_day and savings_target_percent, which the AI already reads in every chat. Marks onboarding_complete.
  • End on a choice: "Set up my budget with buddy" or "Look around first". Every step can be skipped and edited later from a "Money profile" ticket on Profile.

Four decisions before building

The centre button

My recommendation: keep tap for chat and hold for Quick Add, and add the visible "+ Log" pills plus chat quick-log. The alternative is tap for Quick Add and hold for chat, which puts logging first but moves the AI one step back.

Envelope words

Rename "Ready to assign / Assigned / Available" to "Left to budget / Budgeted / Left"? The plain words suit a US and EU audience that mostly hasn't used YNAB.

Default currency

New profiles take the phone's region (USD for a US phone), or stay on INR with a picker? The first matches the launch plan.

Goal money

Should a goal contribution take money out of "Left to budget", the way an envelope does? That makes goals and budget one system, and needs a backend change to how unassigned is worked out.
